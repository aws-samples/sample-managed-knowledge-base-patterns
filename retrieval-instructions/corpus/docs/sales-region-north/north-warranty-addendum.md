## How this addendum works
This addendum applies to AnySmartProduct-5 and AnySmartProduct-6 units bought in Sales Region North. It replaces the warranty term of the global warranty policy for those units. Everything else in the global warranty policy, including what is covered and what is excluded, still applies.

## Sales Region North warranty term
For units bought in Sales Region North, the warranty term is 30 months from the purchase date, for both the AnySmartProduct-5 and the AnySmartProduct-6.

## Examples
- An AnySmartProduct-6 bought in Sales Region North on 2025-01-20 is covered through 2027-07-20.
- An AnySmartProduct-5 bought in Sales Region North on 2023-11-02 was covered through 2026-05-02, so its warranty has ended as of 2026-10-01.

## Units bought elsewhere
A unit bought in another sales region keeps the term of the sales region where it was bought, even if the owner now lives in Sales Region North. Sales Region North support can tell the customer which document applies, and refers the claim to the support team of the sales region of purchase or to the global support contacts.

## Making a claim in Sales Region North
Contact Sales Region North support (see the Sales Region North local support and conventions document) with the purchase date, the serial number and a description of the fault.

## Reconditioned units
This addendum does not apply to reconditioned units.
