## How this addendum works
This addendum applies to AnySmartProduct-5 and AnySmartProduct-6 units bought in Sales Region Central. It replaces the warranty term of the global warranty policy for those units. Coverage and exclusions in the global warranty policy still apply.

## Sales Region Central warranty term
For units bought in Sales Region Central, the warranty term is 36 months from the purchase date, for both the AnySmartProduct-5 and the AnySmartProduct-6.

## Examples
- An AnySmartProduct-5 bought in Sales Region Central on 2024-02-11 is covered through 2027-02-11.
- An AnySmartProduct-6 bought in Sales Region Central on 2023-04-30 was covered through 2026-04-30, so its warranty has ended as of 2026-10-01.

## Units bought elsewhere
A unit bought in another sales region keeps the term of the sales region where it was bought. Sales Region Central support refers those claims to the global support contacts.

## Making a claim in Sales Region Central
Contact Sales Region Central support (see the Sales Region Central local support and conventions document) with the purchase date, the serial number and a description of the fault.

## Reconditioned units
This addendum does not apply to reconditioned units.
