## AnySmartProduct-5 home controller
The AnySmartProduct-5 is a wall-mounted home controller with a touch panel. It connects room sensors, schedules heating and lighting scenes, and shows alerts. It runs on mains power through the supplied adapter and mounts on a standard wall plate.

Key features:
- Touch panel with a dimmable display.
- Built-in radio that connects up to 24 AnySmartProduct-6 room sensors.
- Scene scheduling with daily and weekly programs.
- Local operation: scenes keep running when the home network is down.
- Firmware updates delivered over the home network.

## AnySmartProduct-6 room sensor
The AnySmartProduct-6 is a small battery-powered room sensor. It measures temperature, humidity and motion and reports to an AnySmartProduct-5.

Key features:
- Temperature, humidity and motion sensing.
- Replaceable battery.
- Status light for pairing and low battery.
- Firmware updates delivered through the AnySmartProduct-5.

## Compatibility
The AnySmartProduct-5 works with AnySmartProduct-6 room sensors and with the AnyCompany companion app on phones and tablets. AnyCompany has not tested and does not document use of the AnySmartProduct-5 or AnySmartProduct-6 in vehicles, boats or caravans, and does not publish compatibility with in-vehicle systems.
