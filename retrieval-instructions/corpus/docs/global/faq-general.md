## Which rules apply to my unit?
AnyCompany publishes a global base policy for warranty and returns, and each sales region can publish an addendum that replaces some of the terms. The rules of the sales region where you bought the unit apply. If you are not sure which sales region that is, check your order confirmation or ask support.

## How long is the warranty?
The global base warranty term is 24 months. Your sales region may have a different term in its warranty addendum. Check the addendum for the sales region where you bought the unit.

## How long do I have to return a unit?
The global base return window is 30 days from delivery. Your sales region may have a different return window in its returns addendum.

## Do I pay anything to return an opened unit?
An opened unit carries a restocking fee. The global base fee is listed in the global returns policy. Sales regions with a returns addendum set their own fee.

## Can I return a gift?
Yes. The person who received the gift can start the return with the order number. The refund goes to the original payment method.

## How do I update the firmware?
The AnySmartProduct-5 updates over the home network. Sensors update through the controller. See the public firmware release notes for the latest version.

## How do I reach support?
Use the local support team for your sales region. If your sales region has no local team, use the global support contacts.

## Can I use AnySmartProduct-5 in a car?
AnyCompany does not document or support use in vehicles.

## Can I get a discount for a large order?
AnyCompany does not publish volume discounts. Ask the volume pricing contact in the public price list for a quote.
