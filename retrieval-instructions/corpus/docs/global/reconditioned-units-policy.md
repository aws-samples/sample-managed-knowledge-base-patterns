## What a reconditioned unit is
A reconditioned AnySmartProduct-5 or AnySmartProduct-6 is a unit that was returned, inspected, repaired if needed, and tested by AnyCompany before it was sold again. Reconditioned units are sold with a "Reconditioned" label on the box and on the order confirmation.

## Warranty for reconditioned units
Reconditioned units carry a 6 months warranty from the delivery date. The regional warranty addenda do not apply to reconditioned units, and the global base warranty term does not apply either.

## Returns for reconditioned units
Reconditioned units can be returned within 7 days of delivery. No restocking fee applies. The holiday return extension does not apply to reconditioned units.

## How to tell if your unit is reconditioned
- The order confirmation lists "Reconditioned" next to the product name.
- The serial label has an R at the end of the serial number.

## Claims
Use the same support contacts as for new units. Tell support that the unit is reconditioned so the right terms are applied.
