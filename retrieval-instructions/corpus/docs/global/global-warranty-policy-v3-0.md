## Purpose
This policy sets the global base warranty for AnySmartProduct-5 (home controller) and AnySmartProduct-6 (room sensor). It is the default for every AnyCompany sales region. A sales region can publish a warranty addendum that replaces the term in this policy for units bought in that sales region. When an addendum exists, the addendum wins for units bought there.

## Warranty term
The global base warranty term is 24 months for both AnySmartProduct-5 and AnySmartProduct-6. The term starts on the purchase date shown on the receipt or order confirmation. If no purchase date can be shown, the term starts on the manufacturing date printed on the label under the mounting plate.

The warranty term does not restart when a unit is repaired or replaced. A replacement unit keeps the remaining term of the unit it replaces.

## What the warranty covers
- Defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use.
- Failure of the built-in radio, the touch panel of the AnySmartProduct-5, and the sensing element of the AnySmartProduct-6.
- Firmware faults that cannot be fixed by installing the current public firmware release.

## What the warranty does not cover
- Damage from drops, liquid, fire, or an incorrect power supply.
- Cosmetic wear such as scratches on the housing.
- Units opened or modified by anyone other than an AnyCompany service partner.
- Batteries in the AnySmartProduct-6 after normal depletion. Battery replacement is a consumable item.
- Reconditioned units. Reconditioned units follow the reconditioned units policy instead of this policy.

## How to make a claim
1. Contact the support team listed for the sales region where the unit was bought. The global support contacts document lists the global team for anyone whose sales region has no local team.
2. Provide the purchase date, the serial number, and a short description of the fault.
3. Support confirms whether the unit is inside its warranty term, using the term that applies to the sales region of purchase.
4. If the claim is accepted, AnyCompany repairs or replaces the unit at no charge.

## Checking whether a unit is still covered
Add the warranty term to the purchase date. If today is on or before that end date, the unit is covered. Examples for the global base term:
- Bought 2025-06-01: covered through 2027-06-01.
- Bought 2024-08-20: covered through 2026-08-20, so the warranty has ended as of 2026-10-01.

Support agents should always confirm the sales region of purchase first, because a regional addendum can make the term longer or shorter than the global base.

## Relationship to other documents
- Regional warranty addenda (Sales Region North, Sales Region Central, Sales Region South) replace the term for units bought in those sales regions.
- The global returns policy covers returns for a refund, which is separate from a warranty claim.
- The reconditioned units policy covers reconditioned stock.
