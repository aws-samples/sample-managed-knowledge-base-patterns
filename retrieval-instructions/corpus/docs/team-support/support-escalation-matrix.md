## Escalation codes
| Code | When to use | Goes to | Internal service level |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESC-1 | Customer disputes a warranty or return decision | Global support lead | 2 business days |
| ESC-2 | Firmware fault persists after the latest public firmware | Engineering on-call | 1 business day |
| ESC-3 | Possible safety issue (heat, smell, smoke) | Product safety | Same business day |
| ESC-4 | Data protection request | Legal and compliance | 3 business days |

## How to raise an escalation
1. Write the ticket note first, so the receiving team can read the case.
2. Add the escalation code to the ticket.
3. Tell the customer that the case was passed on and when they will hear back. Do not tell the customer the internal service level as a promise.

## What not to escalate
- Volume pricing or discount questions. Hand these to the volume pricing contact instead.
- Questions about another sales region's terms. Use the global support contacts.

## Closing the loop
The agent who raised the escalation stays the owner of the ticket and updates the customer.
