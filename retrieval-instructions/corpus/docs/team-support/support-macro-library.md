## How to use macros
Macros are short replies that agents adapt to the customer. Always replace the bracketed parts and check the numbers against the document for the sales region of purchase.

## Macro: ask for the sales region of purchase
"Thanks for reaching out. So I can give you the right terms, could you tell me where you bought the unit, or share your order number?"

## Macro: firmware restarts
"Please install the latest public firmware from the AnySmartProduct-5 settings. If your sensor still restarts after the update, reply to this message and we will pass your case to our specialists."

## Macro: volume order
"Thanks for your interest in a larger order. Our volume pricing desk prepares quotes: Mateo Jackson, mateo_jackson@example.com, 555-0144. Please include the products, the number of units and the delivery date you need."

## Macro: out of scope
"I'm sorry, I don't have information about that. Our documents cover the AnySmartProduct-5 and AnySmartProduct-6 in the home. For anything else, please contact your local support team."

## Macro: follow-up
"Just checking in on your case. If we do not hear back within five business days, we will close the ticket. You can reopen it any time by replying."

## Things macros never contain
- Internal escalation codes or service levels.
- Volume discount tiers or percentages. Support agents do not have authority to offer discounts.
- Internal engineering notes.
