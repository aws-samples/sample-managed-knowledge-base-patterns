## Purpose
This playbook tells AnyCompany support agents how to handle a ticket from first contact to closure. It applies to agents in every sales region and in the global support team. It is internal and is not shared with customers.

## Step 1: identify the sales region of purchase
Ask for the order number or the purchase evidence before quoting any term. Warranty terms, return windows and restocking fees differ by sales region, and the sales region of purchase decides which apply. If the customer does not know, look up the order record.

## Step 2: classify the ticket
- Warranty claim: the unit is faulty and inside its warranty term.
- Return: the customer wants a refund inside the return window.
- How-to: setup, pairing, scenes, firmware updates.
- Volume or business order: hand over to the volume pricing contact. Support agents do not quote volume prices or discounts.
- Data protection: forward to legal and compliance through the global support contacts.

## Step 3: check the documents
Use the global policy plus the addendum of the sales region of purchase. Quote the exact number from the document. Do not estimate.

## Step 4: firmware faults
For AnySmartProduct-6 restarts after 5.3.2, ask the customer to install 5.3.3 first. If restarts continue on 5.3.3, raise escalation code ESC-2. Do not share internal engineering notes with customers.

## Step 5: write the ticket note
Every ticket gets a short internal note: what the customer asked, which documents were used, what was decided, and the next action.

## Step 6: escalation
Escalation code ESC-2 is for firmware faults that persist after the latest public firmware. The internal service level for ESC-2 is 1 business day to first engineering response. See the support escalation matrix for the other codes.

## Step 7: closure
Close the ticket when the customer confirms the outcome or after the follow-up period in the support macro library.
