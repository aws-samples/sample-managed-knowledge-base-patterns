## What a legal hold is
A legal hold pauses normal deletion for records that may be needed in a dispute or an official inquiry. During a hold, the retention periods in the data retention notice do not apply to the held records.

## Who can start a hold
Only the legal and compliance team can start or release a hold.

## Steps
1. Open a hold record with the scope: which households, which data types, which dates.
2. Tell the data platform team to pause deletion for that scope.
3. Confirm in writing that deletion is paused.
4. Review the hold every quarter.
5. Release the hold in writing when it is no longer needed. Normal retention resumes from the release date.

## What agents should do
Support and field sales agents do not start holds. If a customer mentions a dispute or an official inquiry, the agent notes it in the ticket and forwards it to legal and compliance.

## Records
Hold records are kept for as long as the hold lasts plus the period set by the legal team.
