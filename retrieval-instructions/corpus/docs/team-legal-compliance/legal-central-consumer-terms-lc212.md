## Purpose
LC-212 records the consumer terms that AnyCompany applies in Sales Region Central, and the reasons behind the Sales Region Central returns addendum. It is an internal legal record. Customer-facing wording lives in the Sales Region Central addenda.

## Return window
LC-212 v1.1 sets the Sales Region Central return window at 45 days from delivery. This is the source of the window in the Sales Region Central returns addendum.

## Warranty term
LC-212 v1.1 requires that the Sales Region Central warranty addendum is not shorter than the global base term. The Sales Region Central addendum meets this requirement.

## Review cycle
LC-212 is reviewed every year by the legal and compliance team. Any change must be reflected in the Sales Region Central addenda before its effective date.

## Risk notes
Applying the global base window in Sales Region Central instead of the addendum would be a compliance issue. Agents in other sales regions must not apply LC-212 to units bought outside Sales Region Central.
