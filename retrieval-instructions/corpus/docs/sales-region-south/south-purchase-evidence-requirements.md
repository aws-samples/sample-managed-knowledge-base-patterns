## When purchase evidence is needed
In Sales Region South, purchase evidence is needed for every return and every warranty claim on an AnySmartProduct-5 or AnySmartProduct-6.

## What counts as purchase evidence
- The original receipt or invoice showing the product, the date and the seller.
- The AnyCompany order confirmation email.
- A gift receipt, for gifts.

## What does not count
- A bank or card statement on its own.
- The manufacturing date on the unit label.
- A photo of the box.

## How to send it
Attach a clear copy to the email to Sales Region South support, or show it when you hand over the unit. Support checks that the product, the date and the seller match the unit.

## If the evidence is missing
Without purchase evidence, Sales Region South support cannot accept the return or the warranty claim. The customer can ask the seller for a copy of the invoice.
