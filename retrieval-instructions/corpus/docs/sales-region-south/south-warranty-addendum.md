## How this addendum works
This addendum applies to AnySmartProduct-5 and AnySmartProduct-6 units bought in Sales Region South. It replaces the warranty term of the global warranty policy for those units. Coverage and exclusions in the global warranty policy still apply. Sales Region South applies the strictest terms of any AnyCompany sales region.

## Sales Region South warranty term
For units bought in Sales Region South, the warranty term is 12 months from the purchase date, for both the AnySmartProduct-5 and the AnySmartProduct-6.

## Purchase evidence
A warranty claim in Sales Region South needs purchase evidence. See the Sales Region South purchase evidence requirements. A manufacturing date on the label is not accepted instead of purchase evidence in Sales Region South.

## Examples
- An AnySmartProduct-6 bought in Sales Region South on 2026-02-14 is covered through 14 Feb 2027.
- An AnySmartProduct-5 bought in Sales Region South on 2025-05-03 was covered through 3 May 2026, so its warranty has ended as of 2026-10-01.

## Units bought elsewhere
A unit bought in another sales region keeps the term of the sales region where it was bought. Sales Region South support refers those claims to the global support contacts and does not quote another sales region's terms.

## Making a claim in Sales Region South
Contact Sales Region South support (see the Sales Region South local support and conventions document) with the purchase evidence, the serial number and a description of the fault.

## Reconditioned units
This addendum does not apply to reconditioned units.
