## How this addendum works
This addendum applies to AnySmartProduct-5 and AnySmartProduct-6 units bought in Sales Region South. It replaces the return window and the restocking fee of the global returns policy for those units. The purchase-region rule of the global returns policy still applies.

## Sales Region South return window
Units bought in Sales Region South can be returned within 14 days of the delivery date. Only unopened units in their original sealed packaging can be returned. Purchase evidence is required for every return (see the Sales Region South purchase evidence requirements).

## Sales Region South restocking fee
A restocking fee of $25.00 applies to every return in Sales Region South, including unopened units.

## Holiday return extension
The holiday return extension does not apply in Sales Region South. The 14-day window applies all year, including purchases in November and December.

## Opened units
Opened units cannot be returned for a refund in Sales Region South. A faulty opened unit can be assessed under the Sales Region South warranty addendum instead.

## Starting a return
Contact Sales Region South support with the order number and the purchase evidence.

## Units bought in another sales region
Returns of units bought in another sales region follow that sales region's rules. Sales Region South support does not quote those rules and refers the customer to the global support contacts.
