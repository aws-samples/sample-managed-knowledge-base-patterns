## Who can use this document
Only AnyCompany field sales representatives may quote these tiers, and only to business customers in a written quote. Support agents and partners must not quote them.

## Volume discount tiers (AnySmartProduct-5 and AnySmartProduct-6)
Discounts apply to the public list price, per order, for a single delivery.

| Units in one order | Discount off list price |
|---|---|
| 1 to 9 | none |
| 10 to 49 | 4% |
| 50 to 199 | 9% |
| 200 or more | 15% |

## Regional caps
Some sales regions cap the discount. Sales Region South has a cap; see the field sales Sales Region South discount cap addendum. Where a cap applies, quote the lower of the tier and the cap.

## Worked example
A customer in Sales Region North orders 120 AnySmartProduct-5 units at the list price of the public price list. The order falls in the 50 to 199 tier, so the discount is 9% off list price.

## Approvals
Quotes inside the tiers do not need approval. Anything above a tier goes to the deal desk (see the field sales deal desk process).
