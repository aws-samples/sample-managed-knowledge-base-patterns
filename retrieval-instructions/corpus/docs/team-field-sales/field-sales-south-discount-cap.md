## Cap
For orders delivered in Sales Region South, the volume discount is capped at 7% off list price, whatever the unit count. Quote the lower of the standard tier and the cap.

## Examples
- 30 units delivered in Sales Region South: tier gives 4%, cap is higher, so quote 4%.
- 120 units delivered in Sales Region South: tier would give more than the cap, so quote 7%.
- 250 units delivered in Sales Region South: quote 7%.

## Exceptions
There are no exceptions through the deal desk for Sales Region South. The cap is set by Sales Region South commercial rules.

## Why this exists
Sales Region South applies the strictest commercial terms of any AnyCompany sales region.
