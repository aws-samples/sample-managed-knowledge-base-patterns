## When to use the deal desk
Use the deal desk when a business customer asks for terms outside the standard discount tiers, for example a larger discount, split deliveries, or extended payment terms.

## How to submit a deal
1. Write a deal brief: customer need, the requested terms, the standard tier that would apply, and the sales region of delivery.
2. Send it to the volume pricing desk (Mateo Jackson, mateo_jackson@example.com).
3. The deal desk replies with approved terms or a counter-offer.

## What the deal desk checks
- The standard tier for the unit count.
- Any regional cap for the sales region of delivery.
- Whether the customer has an open order or a past dispute.

## Writing the customer quote
Quote only approved terms. State the unit count, the list price, the discount and the source document. Never promise terms before the deal desk approves them.

## Records
Keep the deal brief and the approval in the customer record.
