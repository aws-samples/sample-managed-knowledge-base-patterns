# AnyCompany Retail FY2026 Business Review: Q2 and Q3

Prepared by Arnav Desai, Planning, for the CFO, Nikki Wolf, on 2025-11-14. Covers fiscal Q2 (May to July 2025) and fiscal Q3 (August to October 2025) of FY2026. The numbers are in the final FY2026 P&L workbook; this review explains what drove the variances and does not restate them.

## Fiscal Q2

- Engineering: the Platform cost center (CC-3010) brought in contractors to finish the data platform migration ahead of the Product-12 launch. The contracts ended in July.
- Customer Support: Tier 1 Support (CC-5010) added contractors and paid overtime to handle the higher contact volume after the Product-7 launch.
- Sales: Field Sales (CC-1010) travel for the regional kickoff events in May.
- Finance: spend was lower than planned because two open analyst roles in Planning (CC-6020) stayed vacant through the quarter.
- Marketing and Operations were close to plan.

## Fiscal Q3

- Sales: Inside Sales (CC-1020) spent less than planned on salaries because of open headcount. Hiring resumes in Q4.
- Operations: Fulfillment (CC-4010) facilities cost rose after the warehouse lease renewal at 123 Any Street, Any Town, USA. Operations asked for a budget revision, which was not approved (see the Q3 close memo).
- Marketing: Demand Generation (CC-2020) ran the Product-12 launch campaign. The approved budget revision in the Q3 close memo covers it.
- Engineering: Product Engineering (CC-3020) paid a software license true-up. The hiring pause lowered the Q3 budget through a revision, but did not reduce spend by the same amount.
- Customer Support and Finance were close to plan.

## Outlook

Q4 planning assumes the Inside Sales hires start in November and that Platform contractor spend stays at the Q3 level.
