# Q3 Revenue Forecast

AnyCompany — Finance team. All figures are fictional.

## Summary

Projected Q3 revenue is 4.2 million dollars, up 11 percent quarter over quarter.
Subscription revenue accounts for 3.1 million of that total, with professional
services making up the remainder.

## Assumptions

- Renewal rate holds at 92 percent.
- Two enterprise deals in late-stage negotiation close within the quarter.
- No change to list pricing.

## Risks

The forecast is sensitive to the two enterprise deals. If both slip to Q4,
projected revenue falls to 3.6 million dollars, roughly flat against Q2.

## Headcount

Finance is budgeted for two additional analysts in Q3, bringing the team to nine.
