# AnyCompany Employee Handbook

General reference for all staff. All content is fictional.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone. Outside those
hours, asynchronous written updates are preferred over meetings.

## Expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days. Receipts are required for any single item over 25
dollars. Travel booked more than 14 days in advance does not require additional
approval.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, an external display, and a 500 dollar allowance
for a desk and chair. The replacement cycle for laptops is three years.

## Time off

Paid time off accrues at 1.75 days per month. Unused days carry over to the
following year up to a maximum of ten days.
