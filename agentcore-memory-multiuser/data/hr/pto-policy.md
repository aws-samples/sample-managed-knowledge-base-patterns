# AnyCompany Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

AnyCompany provides paid time off to all full-time employees. Accrual and carry-over rules depend on the office where the employee is based, because local labor law differs.

## Accrual by office

- Seattle (US): 20 days of PTO per year, accrued at 1.67 days per month. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next calendar year.
- Frankfurt (Germany): 30 days of PTO per year, granted in full on January 1. By German law, unused days must be taken by March 31 of the following year, after which they expire.
- Toronto (Canada): 15 days of PTO per year in the first two years of service, 20 days thereafter. Up to 10 unused days carry over.

## Requesting time off

Submit requests in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of three or more consecutive days. Your manager approves requests in the HR portal. For absences of one or two days, one week of notice is sufficient.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the calendar of the office you are assigned to. Frankfurt employees observe Hessian public holidays. Seattle employees observe the US federal holiday schedule plus the day after Thanksgiving.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Seattle employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Frankfurt employees are covered under the German statutory sick pay scheme and should submit a doctor's certificate (Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung) from the third day of absence.
