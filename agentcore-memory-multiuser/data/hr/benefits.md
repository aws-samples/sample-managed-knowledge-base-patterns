# AnyCompany Employee Benefits Overview

Benefits vary by country of employment. This document summarizes the main programs. Details and enrollment are in the HR portal under Benefits.

## Health insurance

- United States (Seattle): AnyCompany offers three medical plans through a regional health insurer (Gold PPO, Silver PPO, and a High Deductible Health Plan with an HSA). AnyCompany covers 90 percent of the employee premium and 75 percent of dependent premiums. Dental and vision are included with every plan.
- Germany (Frankfurt): Employees are enrolled in statutory health insurance (gesetzliche Krankenversicherung) or may opt for private insurance if their salary exceeds the statutory threshold. AnyCompany pays the employer share as required by law and additionally offers a supplementary dental plan through a private insurer.
- Canada (Toronto): AnyCompany provides extended health and dental coverage through a group benefits provider on top of provincial health care.

## Retirement

- Seattle: 401(k) with a plan administrator. AnyCompany matches 100 percent of the first 4 percent of salary contributed. Vesting is immediate.
- Frankfurt: Company pension (betriebliche Altersvorsorge) through an insurance provider with a 20 percent employer top-up on employee contributions.
- Toronto: Group RRSP with a group benefits provider and a 4 percent employer match.

## Learning budget

Every employee receives a learning budget of 2,000 USD or equivalent per year for courses, conferences, and certifications. Requests are approved by your manager in the HR portal. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Commuter benefits

Seattle employees can enroll in a pre-tax ORCA transit card. Frankfurt employees receive a subsidized Deutschlandticket. Toronto employees can enroll in a pre-tax PRESTO card.
