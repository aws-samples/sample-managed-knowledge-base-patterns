# AnyCompany New Hire Onboarding Checklist

Welcome to AnyCompany. This checklist covers your first two weeks.

## Before day one

- Complete your background check and sign your offer letter in the HR portal.
- IT ships your laptop to your home address or holds it at your office front desk. Choose your operating system (macOS or Windows) in the pre-boarding form. Engineering roles default to macOS.

## Day one

- Attend the 09:00 orientation session (local office time) with the People team.
- Set up your laptop following the IT Laptop Setup Guide. Enroll in AnyCompany SSO and register a hardware security key or the authenticator app for multi-factor authentication.
- Join your team's chat channels. Every employee is added to #announcements and #it-help automatically.

## First week

- Complete the mandatory security awareness training and code of conduct training in the HR portal.
- Enroll in benefits within 30 days of your start date.
- Schedule a one-on-one with your manager to agree on your 30-60-90 day plan.

## First two weeks

- Engineers: complete the Platform team's onboarding path in the developer portal, get access to the deployment pipelines, and add yourself to your team's on-call schedule in the paging tool as a shadow.
- Sales and go-to-market roles: complete CRM training and shadow three customer calls.

## Buddy program

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for the first 90 days.
