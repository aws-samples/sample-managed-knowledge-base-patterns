# AnyCompany Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

AnyCompany is a hybrid company. Most teams are expected in the office three days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday as anchor days when the whole team is present.

## Fully remote arrangements

Employees may apply for a fully remote arrangement after 12 months of service. Applications go through the HR portal and require manager and HR business partner approval. Fully remote employees must live in a country where AnyCompany has a legal entity: the United States, Germany, or Canada.

## Working from another country

Working from a country other than your employment country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and must be pre-approved by HR because of tax and immigration rules. Frankfurt-based employees who want to work from another EU country for longer must consult the HR business partner for Germany, as an A1 certificate may be required.

## Home office equipment

AnyCompany provides a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD (Seattle, Toronto) or 450 EUR (Frankfurt) for a desk, chair, or monitor. Laptops and peripherals are provided by IT and are not covered by the stipend. Submit receipts through the expense tool within 60 days of purchase.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your office's local time zone. Cross-office meetings between Seattle and Frankfurt should be scheduled between 08:00 and 10:00 Pacific time, which is 17:00 to 19:00 Central European time.
