# AnyCompany Travel and Expense Policy

All business expenses are submitted through the expense tool and must include an itemized receipt for any amount over 25 USD or 25 EUR.

## Travel booking

Book flights and hotels through the AnyCompany travel portal. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Flights over six hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires VP approval.

## Per diem and meals

AnyCompany does not use a per diem. Reasonable meal expenses are reimbursed with receipts. Guideline maximums per day are 75 USD in the United States, 60 EUR in Germany, and 90 CAD in Canada. Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment with a client name on the expense.

## Software and subscriptions

Software under 100 USD per year may be expensed directly with manager approval. Anything above that threshold, or any software that processes AnyCompany data, must go through the IT software request process so that a security review can be completed.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle. Submit expenses within 60 days of the transaction date. Expenses older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Conference attendance

Conference registration is paid from your learning budget. Travel and accommodation for approved conferences are paid from the team travel budget, not the learning budget.
