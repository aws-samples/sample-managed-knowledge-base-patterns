# AnyCompany Sales Commission Plan (FY2026)

This plan applies to quota-carrying account executives in the Sales organization. It is confidential to the Sales organization.

## Commission rates

- Below 50% of quarterly quota: no commission.
- 50% to 100% of quota: 8% of booked annual contract value (ACV).
- Above 100% of quota (accelerator): 12% of booked ACV on the amount above quota.
- Multi-year deals: commission is paid on the first-year ACV only.

## Office-specific rules

- Frankfurt: commission is paid monthly with payroll, as required by the local works agreement.
- Seattle and Toronto: commission is paid quarterly, in the month after the quarter closes.

## Clawback

If a customer cancels within 90 days of signature, the commission on that deal is recovered from the next payout.

## SPIFFs

The Sales leadership team may announce short incentive programs (SPIFFs) for specific products. SPIFF payouts are listed separately on the commission statement and are not subject to the accelerator.
