# Example Corp Travel Policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days
before departure. Bookings made inside 14 days require written approval from a director.

## Allowances

- Domestic flights: economy class.
- Flights longer than 8 hours: premium economy permitted.
- Hotels: up to 250 USD per night in tier 1 cities, 180 USD elsewhere.
- Meals: 75 USD per day, receipts required above 25 USD.

## Expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days of trip completion. Expenses submitted after 60 days are
not reimbursed except in documented exceptional circumstances.
