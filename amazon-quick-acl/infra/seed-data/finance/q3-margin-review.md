# Q3 Margin Review (Finance - Restricted)

## Summary

Gross margin for Q3 came in at 61.4 percent, 220 basis points below plan. The shortfall is
concentrated in the hardware resale line, where component costs rose faster than the
contracted price adjustments allowed us to pass through.

## Drivers

- Hardware resale margin fell from 24 percent to 17 percent year over year.
- Support renewals came in 4 percent above plan, partially offsetting the decline.
- One-off migration credits of 1.2 million USD were recognized in the quarter.

## Actions

1. Renegotiate the top five component contracts before the Q4 close.
2. Move the hardware resale line to a cost-plus pricing model in the next contract cycle.
3. Hold discretionary spend in the hardware business unit flat through Q4.
